Hotels With Bathtubs · 25 Oct 2026 · For approval. This replaces plan v2. Data sources: Google Search Console (last 90 days), your own click log (since 20 Oct), Lighthouse tests run today on the live site, and a review of the code and the built pages. Nothing in this plan has been started.

The goal, in one line. Become the best place on the web to find a hotel room with a private bathtub or in-room jacuzzi, for romantic and luxury travellers, with the United States as the main growth market and India as the strong existing base, and send those travellers to Booking.com, Agoda and other partners ready to book.

2. Who we are building for (personas)

Built from your real search terms in Search Console and the click log. Each persona lists what they type, what they need to see before they click "Book", and what stops them.

P1 · "The Anniversary Planner" — United States (primary target)
Couple, 28–45, planning an anniversary, birthday or proposal weekend in a big US city (New York, Las Vegas, Chicago, Orlando, Miami, LA). Budget roughly $250–600 a night. Browses on the phone in the evening, often books on desktop later. Already uses Booking.com or Expedia.
Searches: "hotels with jacuzzi in room nyc", "chicago hotels with jacuzzi suites", "honeymoon suites with jacuzzi in room", "best hotel bathtubs nyc".
Needs: a photo of the actual tub in the actual room; the exact room type to book; is the tub inside the room or in the bathroom; price range; neighbourhood; reassurance it is real.
Stops them: stock photos, vague "spa" claims, a tub that turns out to be in a shared spa, slow pages, clutter.
P2 · "The Weekend Romantic" — United States
Couple, 25–40, wants a drive-to escape: Poconos, Gatlinburg, upstate New York, Lake Tahoe, Scottsdale. Hot tub on a balcony or in the suite. Often considers cabins and Airbnb.
Searches: "romantic getaways with hot tubs", "hot tub getaways near me for couples", "gatlinburg hotels with jacuzzi in room", "champagne hot tub poconos".
Needs: "near [my city]" lists, private outdoor tubs, weekend availability, mixing hotels and private stays.
Stops them: no regional "near me" pages; city-only structure.
P3 · "The Luxury Couple" — India (existing strong base)
Married or unmarried couple, 24–38, metro cities, anniversary or staycation. Privacy and couple-friendliness matter as much as the tub. ₹6,000–25,000 a night. Mobile-only. Uses MakeMyTrip, Agoda, Booking.com.
Searches: "hotels with bathtub in kolkata for couples", "couple friendly hotels with bathtub in mumbai", "bathtub hotel in jaipur for couples", "bathtub hotel near me".
Needs: couple-friendly confirmation (local ID policy), real tub photo, price in rupees, MakeMyTrip option.
Stops them: unclear couple policy, no MakeMyTrip link (only 24 of 1,548 India hotels have one).
P4 · "The Soak Seeker" — global, smaller but loyal
Solo traveller or older couple who simply wants a proper deep bath after travel, or needs an accessible tub. Searches "deep soaking tub hotel", "accessible bathtub in hotel", "ada tub hotel".
Needs: tub type and size, accessibility details. Opportunity: almost no competitor serves this well, and we already get impressions for it.

9. Technical platform

  • Build on Cloudflare's cloud (from plan v2): you push, Cloudflare builds; every branch gets a private preview. Hotel data reaches the build as a snapshot in R2, not through your database password.
  • Images on your own domain (for example images.hotelswithbathtubs.com) instead of r2.dev, with cached phone/tablet/desktop sizes.
  • Patch upgrades: Next.js 16.4.3 (two critical advisories) and markdown-it.
  • Automatic checks on every push: type-check, lint, link audit and a page-weight budget.
