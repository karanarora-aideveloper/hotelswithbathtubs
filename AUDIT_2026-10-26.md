# Audit: 26 October 2026 (after the D6–D8 production release)

Live site checked: https://www.hotelswithbathtubs.com (production build `ec0141b`, cache purged).
Sources: Google Search Console (26 Sep–24 Oct), phone Lighthouse 12.8 on 4 pages, a crawl of internal links from 51 sample pages, the data snapshot in `.cache/all-hotels.json`, and ESLint.

## Rules for whoever fixes these

- **Do not touch** `.cache/all-hotels.json`, `scripts/collect-partner-photos.mjs` or the `partnerImages` field. A photo collection run is writing them in the background. The run is finished when `.cache/partner-photos.log` ends with `Done.`.
- **Mac limit:** at most 2 scripts with 2 browser workers on the Mac. Do not start another Playwright job while the photo run is going.
- **No affiliate changes.** Not all affiliates are approved yet.
- Work on a branch and push it for a Cloudflare preview. The owner approves before anything is merged to `main`. Builds run on Cloudflare's cloud, never locally. After a production deploy, the owner purges the Cloudflare cache.
- **Decisions already made:** D10 and D11 keep all hotel pages indexed. D12 leaves the 152 broken-word slugs alone. Don't reopen them.

Each item has: **what's wrong**, **where**, **fix**, and **how to check it's fixed**.

## P0: bugs hurting search right now

### 1. `sitemap_index.xml` redirects to itself forever
- **Wrong:** `/sitemap_index.xml` returns a 302 to `/sitemap_index.xml`, in a loop. Search Console shows 1 error on this sitemap.
- **Where:** `scripts/redirects.mjs:91-92` generates `public/_redirects:80-81`.
- **Fix:** delete both rules. `public/sitemap_index.xml` already exists and is correct.
- **Check:** `curl -sI https://<preview>/sitemap_index.xml` returns `200` with XML content.

### 2. The country Georgia (Tbilisi, 18 hotels) hub is unreachable
- **Wrong:** `/georgia` sends a 301 to `/usa/georgia`, the US state. The `/destinations` "Browse by country" link and the Tbilisi breadcrumbs both send people and Google to the wrong country.
- **Where:** `scripts/redirects.mjs:42-43`.
- **Fix:** remove those two rules so `/georgia` renders the country hub. The US state already lives at `/usa/georgia`.
- **Check:** `/georgia` returns 200 with a Georgia (country) H1. `/usa/georgia` still returns 200.

### 3. Every page fires 404s for Next.js navigation files (`__next.*.txt`)
- **Wrong:** visible links make the browser fetch `/<path>/__next._tree.txt?_rsc=…`, and every one 404s.
  - The home page fires 15 of these, and each returns the full 66 KB HTML 404 page, about 300 KB wasted per visit.
  - Lighthouse flags `errors-in-console` on every page because of them.
- **Why:** `scripts/build-cloudflare.mjs:166-169` deletes all `__next.*` files to stay under Cloudflare Pages' 20,000-file limit. The client router still asks for them.
- **Fix (pick one, test on the preview):**
  - (a) Turn off prefetching for mass links: `prefetch={false}` on `<Link>` in card grids, footer, header, breadcrumbs and destination lists. A small wrapper component makes this easy.
  - **Plus**, in `functions/_middleware.ts`, answer any path containing `/__next.` with an empty-body 404 before `context.next()`, so a stray request costs a few bytes.
  - (b) Longer term: move to Workers static assets, which allow far more files on the paid plan, and keep the files.
- **Check:** load the home page and a city page in Chrome DevTools. No `__next.` 404s, and clicking a card and pressing back still works. Lighthouse `errors-in-console` passes.

### 4. Placeholder location text shows on cards and in Google schema
- **Wrong:**
  - 2,674 of 4,469 hotels have `neighborhood = "Prime <City> Enclave"`, for example "Prime New York Enclave". It shows under the hotel name on cards (`CityHotelsClient.tsx:1456`).
  - 4,288 hotels have `streetAddress` built from that placeholder. It goes into the `LodgingBusiness` schema as the street address (`src/app/[country]/[city]/[hotel]/page.tsx:282`).
  - It looks fake to visitors and gives Google false structured data.
- **Fix:**
  - Code (now): treat a value matching `/^Prime .* Enclave/i`, or a `streetAddress` with no digit, as missing. Leave `streetAddress` out of the schema; `addressLocality` and `addressCountry` are enough. On cards, show `City, Country` instead.
  - Data (later): fill real neighbourhoods and addresses from the Booking.com page during a future collection run.
- **Check:** The Greenwich Hotel card shows "New York, USA". The hotel page JSON-LD has no `streetAddress`. Rich Results Test passes.

### 5. Old and alias URLs that Google still shows now 404
- **Wrong:**
  - `/kochi/` still gets impressions (85, average position 6.4), but `/kochi` returns 404.
  - `/india/bengaluru` returns 404 (the data uses `bangalore`).
  - Uppercase URLs such as `/USA/New-York` return 404.
- **Where:** `functions/_middleware.ts` only rescues 3-part hotel URLs.
- **Fix:**
  - On a 404 with one path part that matches a known city slug (from `locations.json`), 301 to `/<country>/<city>`.
  - 301 any path containing uppercase letters to its lowercase form.
  - Add alias rules (`bengaluru`→`bangalore`, `gurugram`→`gurgaon`, `bombay`→`mumbai` and similar) in `scripts/redirects.mjs`.
- **Check:** `/kochi`, `/india/bengaluru` and `/USA/New-York` each do a single 301 to a 200 page.

## P1: SEO improvements (ranked by traffic upside)

### 6. India city pages rank on page 1 but almost nobody clicks
- Search Console, last 28 days:

  | Page | Impressions | CTR | Position |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `/india/delhi` | 1,065 | 0.85% | 11.3 |
  | `/india/mumbai` | 1,026 | 0.78% | 11.0 |
  | `/india/chennai` | 814 | 0.61% | 10.6 |
  | `/india/indore` | 497 | 0.20% | 11.6 |
  | `/india/agra` | 404 | 0.50% | 10.3 |
  | `/india/coimbatore` | 354 | 0.28% | 10.0 |
  | `/india/pune` | 301 | 0.33% | 12.6 |

  India brings 18.6k impressions at average position 10.3. The US brings 6.3k at position 15.7.
- **Wrong:** the meta descriptions are generic travel prose ("Delhi, the historic capital, offers…"). They give no count, no price and no proof. Title wording also varies by city: New York's title says "Jacuzzi in Room" while the page and the searches are about soaking tubs and bathtubs.
- **Fix:** one title and description template for city pages that leads with the searched words, the count and a reason to click. For example:
  - Title: `Hotels with Bathtub in Delhi: 38 Verified Rooms (2026)`
  - Description: `38 Delhi hotels where the room itself has a bathtub or jacuzzi, checked one by one. Prices from ₹X/night. Couple-friendly picks, exact room type to book.`
  - Use local currency for India. Make the New York title about soaking tubs and bathtubs.
- **Check:** compare CTR for these 7 pages in Search Console 28 days after deploy.

### 7. Titles too long or with the brand twice
- The layout template adds ` | Hotels With Bathtubs` (`src/app/layout.tsx:34`), and several pages add the brand again.
  - `/contact` reads "…| Hotels With Bathtubs | Hotels With Bathtubs".
  - The same happens on `/editorial-policy`, `/destinations` (90 characters), `/hostels` (92), `/couple-friendly` (95), `/hotels-with-hot-tub-in-room` (82), `/soaking-tub-hotels` (88) and `/redesign`.
- Blog post titles reach 100 characters, for example `/blog/hotels-with-soaking-tubs-nyc`.
- About 3,000 of 4,469 hotel titles are over 60 characters: name + " — " + tub type + " in " + city, plus the suffix (`[hotel]/page.tsx:192-218`). 609 hotel names are over 45 characters, mostly holiday-rental listings ("Ganga Vista | Luxury 2-Bedroom Apartment • Walk to…").
- **Fix:**
  - Remove the brand from page-level titles that already have it.
  - Hotel pages: use `{name} – Room with {tubType}, {city}` and, when the name is longer than 40 characters, shorten it at the first `|`, `•` or `-`.
  - Keep meta descriptions under 155 characters. Hotel descriptions run up to 179, and "features Deep Soaking Bathtub in Executive Suite with Deep Tub" reads like a template; rewrite it as a sentence.
- **Check:** a crawl of the sitemap shows no title over 65 characters and no doubled brand.

### 8. Blog posts compete with each other (and with city pages)
These pairs and groups target the same search:
- **NYC:** `nyc-hotels-with-soaking-tubs` and `hotels-with-soaking-tubs-nyc` (both around position 12–14).
- **Delhi:** `hotel-with-bathtub-in-delhi`, `hotels-with-bathtub-in-delhi` and the city page `/india/delhi`.
- **Lucknow:** `hotel-with-bathtub-in-lucknow` and `hotels-with-bathtub-in-lucknow`.
- **Las Vegas:** `hotels-with-jacuzzi-in-room-las-vegas` and `las-vegas-hotels-with-jacuzzi-in-room`.
- **Dubai:** `dubai-hotels-with-private-jacuzzi`, `hotels-with-private-jacuzzi-in-room-dubai` and `top-5-luxury-hotels-with-private-hot-tubs-in-dubai`.
- **Kolkata:** `hotel-with-bathtub-in-kolkata`, `kolkata-hotels-with-bathtub-couples-guide` and `kolkata-jacuzzi-hotels-romantic-staycation`.
- **Others:** Bangalore ×2, Munnar ×2, Lonavala ×3, Ooty ×2, Manali ×3, Bhopal ×2, Rishikesh ×2, Venice ×2, Barcelona ×2, Bali ×2, Miami ×2, Los Angeles ×2, San Francisco ×2, London ×3.

**Fix (needs the owner's OK on which post survives):**
- In each group, keep the post with more clicks, merge the useful content into it, and 301 the others to it (add the rules in `scripts/redirects.mjs`).
- A blog post must not target the plain "hotels with bathtub in {city}" search; that's the city page's job. Give posts a different angle ("for honeymoon", "under ₹5,000", "with a view") and a link to the city page using the exact search phrase.

**Check:** one URL per keyword. Watch average position for "hotels with soaking tubs nyc" in Search Console.

### 9. 20 blog posts share a photo of The Leela Palace New Delhi as their main image
- **Affected posts** include `nyc-hotels-with-soaking-tubs`, `best-hotels-with-jacuzzi-in-room-nyc`, `romantic-hotels-with-private-hot-tubs-near-nyc-upstate`, `miami-hotels-with-deep-soaking-tubs-and-jacuzzis` and `hotel-with-bathtub-in-lucknow`.
- That image is the `BlogPosting` schema image and the social share image, so a New York post shows a Delhi hotel.
- **Fix:** set each post's `image` to a photo of a hotel the post actually features (from that hotel's R2 images). Find them with: blog `image` contains `leela-palace-new-delhi`.
- **Check:** no post outside Delhi uses a Delhi image.

### 10. Small on-page gaps
- **No `og:image` (share picture):** `/hostels`, `/couple-friendly`, `/contact`, `/editorial-policy`, `/hotels-with-hot-tub-in-room`, `/soaking-tub-hotels`.
- **No H1:** `/matchmaker`.
- **No structured data:** `/destinations`. Add `BreadcrumbList` and `CollectionPage`.
- **Broken internal links:** `/india/mahipalpur` (404) in `/blog/delhi-mahipalpur-hotels-with-bathtub-jacuzzi`. Point it to `/india/delhi`. Also a Cloudflare `/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection` link in `/blog/hotel-with-bathtub-in-bhopal` (an email address in the post body).
- **Grammar on single-hotel pages:** pages with one hotel say "Top 1 Stays", "1 Top Suites" and "Discover 1+ verified suites" (`/india/samode`, `/usa/new-york/jacuzzi`). These are noindexed, but fix the plural wording anyway.

### 11. Google doesn't know `/usa/new-york` exists
- URL Inspection reports "URL is unknown to Google", yet the page is in `sitemap.xml`. Earlier it showed "Discovered – not indexed".
- **Owner action:** in Search Console, open URL Inspection for `https://www.hotelswithbathtubs.com/usa/new-york` and click "Request indexing". Do the same for the top 10 US city pages.
- **Code:** make sure the home page links to `/usa/new-york` high on the page, as a plain `<a>` in the HTML, not only inside a client-side list.

12. **Images 3–5× bigger than shown.** Cards about 180 px wide download 1,000 px images: 351 KB of waste on New York, 158 KB on Home. Generate a 400 px version of each R2 image (for example `name-400.webp`) and use `srcset`/`sizes` on cards. Booking.com photos already use `max500`.
13. **Eight font files (about 250 KB).** Cut to 2 font families with 1–2 weights each. This is already in the plan as "two fonts" (phase 1).
14. **Google Tag Manager: 73 KB of unused JavaScript.** Load gtag with `strategy="lazyOnload"`, or after the first interaction.
15. **The prefetch 404s from item 3:** about 300 KB per home-page visit.
16. **`/api/geo` is called twice** on the home page (`LocationSelector.tsx`). Call it once and cache the result in `sessionStorage`.
17. **Heavy HTML.** City pages are 400–480 KB of HTML, `/india` is 535 KB with 487 links, and `/destinations` is 453 KB. Hotel data is sent twice (in the HTML and again in the page data). Send cards only the fields they render.

**Check:** phone Lighthouse of 75 or more on all four pages, LCP under 4 s.

## Content gaps (for planning, not bugs)

- 1,935 hotel descriptions are under 25 words, and 98 hotels share a description with another hotel. Thin, near-duplicate hotel pages are the main risk now that all hotel pages stay indexed (D10).
- 1,438 hotels have 0–1 photos of our own. The Booking.com photo run will cover those with a Booking.com link.
- 567 hotels have holiday-rental-style names (BHK, "sleeps 10", apartment). They make long titles and look different from the "luxury hotel" positioning for US visitors.
- Strongest US blog openings in Search Console: NYC soaking tubs (654 + 168 impressions across two competing posts), NYC jacuzzi (458 impressions, 0.22% CTR, position 16.9), Orlando jacuzzi (361, 0.28%), and the Los Angeles city page (322 impressions, position 28.7).
