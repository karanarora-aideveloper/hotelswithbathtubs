# Top 7 Romantic Hotels with Bathtubs in Kolkata for Couples: Expert Verified Guide

Kolkata, the City of Joy, is steeped in heritage, colonial architecture, and passionate romance. Whether you are celebrating an anniversary, planning a weekend staycation, or searching for a peaceful romantic getaway, booking a hotel room with a **private in-room bathtub or jacuzzi** elevates your entire experience.

However, not all luxury hotels in Kolkata have bathtubs in every room category. To prevent disappointment at check-in, our team has cross-verified the top properties across MakeMyTrip, Agoda, and Booking.com. Here are the best verified hotels with bathtubs in Kolkata.

---

### 1. The Westin Kolkata Rajarhat
Located in New Town / Rajarhat, The Westin offers panoramic skyline views and state-of-the-art modern luxury.
* **The Bathtub Experience:** The Deluxe Suites and Presidential Suites feature expansive, freestanding soaking tubs situated right beside floor-to-ceiling glass windows overlooking the Rajarhat cityscape.
* **Why Couples Love It:** Heavenly beds, rooftop dining at 31/32, and deep marble soaking tubs with premium white tea bath amenities.
* **Best For:** Modern luxury lovers and honeymooners.

---

### 2. ITC Sonar & ITC Royal Bengal (EM Bypass)
This iconic twin-property complex along the EM Bypass represents the pinnacle of regal Bengali hospitality.
* **The Bathtub Experience:** The Luxury and Presidential suites boast grand Italian marble bathrooms featuring deep sunken bathtubs, complete with plush bathrobes and forest-infused bath salts.
* **Why Couples Love It:** Secluded water pavilions, world-renowned restaurants (Peshawri, Ottimo), and absolute privacy.
* **Best For:** Traditional opulent romance and anniversary celebrations.

---

### 3. JW Marriott Hotel Kolkata (EM Bypass)
JW Marriott Kolkata is a hotspot for contemporary elegance, outstanding service, and vibrant dining.
* **The Bathtub Experience:** The Executive Rooms and Suites come equipped with spacious bathtubs, mood lighting, and separate walk-in rain showers.
* **Why Couples Love It:** Infinity pool overlooking the city, luxury spa therapies at Quan Spa, and late-night room service.
* **Best For:** Premium staycations with lively nightlife and dining.

---

### 4. Hyatt Regency Kolkata (Salt Lake)
Situated in the lush surroundings of Salt Lake, Hyatt Regency offers a peaceful resort-style ambiance away from the crowded city center.
* **The Bathtub Experience:** Regency Suites and Premier King rooms feature deep square-shaped soaking tubs framed by polished granite and warm ambient lighting.
* **Why Couples Love It:** Sprawling landscaped gardens, outdoor swimming pool, and romantic poolside candlelit dinners.
* **Best For:** Peaceful weekend retreats without leaving the city.

---

### 5. Calcutta Bungalow (Shyambazar)
For couples who appreciate heritage architecture, Calcutta Bungalow is a restored 1920s townhouse that takes you back in time.
* **The Bathtub Experience:** Selected heritage suites feature handcrafted vintage clawfoot bathtubs with brass fittings and artisanal herbal bath essentials.
* **Why Couples Love It:** Vintage charm, quiet courtyards, and personalized boutique attention.
* **Best For:** Culture enthusiasts and vintage aesthetics.

---

### 6. The Oberoi Grand (New Market / Esplanade)
Known affectionately as the "Grande Dame of Chowringhee", The Oberoi Grand is an oasis of colonial tranquility in the heart of Kolkata.
* **The Bathtub Experience:** Luxury Suites and Premier Rooms offer classic porcelain bathtubs surrounded by Burma teak woodwork and marble.
* **Why Couples Love It:** Lush central courtyard pool, heritage palm trees, and Oberoi's signature discreet hospitality.
* **Best For:** Timeless elegance and couples seeking heritage luxury.

---

### 7. Vivanta Kolkata EM Bypass
A chic, design-forward property suited for couples seeking modern amenities at an accessible luxury price point.
* **The Bathtub Experience:** Deluxe Allure suites offer ergonomic deep tubs designed for full-body immersion after exploring the city.
* **Why Couples Love It:** Rooftop pool, contemporary aesthetics, and couple-friendly staff.
* **Best For:** Trendy couples and budget-conscious luxury seekers.

---

## 3 Tips for Booking Bathtub Rooms in Kolkata

1. **Verify the Exact Room Category:** Standard or Deluxe base rooms often only feature standing showers. Always reserve the **Suite**, **Executive King**, or specific **Bathtub/Jacuzzi Suite** tier.
2. **Request Rose Petals & Bath Salts in Advance:** Most luxury properties like The Westin and ITC Royal Bengal offer romance packages with customized bath setups if contacted 24 hours prior to check-in.
3. **Check Couple Policies:** All 4-star and 5-star properties in Kolkata welcome unmarried couples with valid government ID (Passport, Driving License, Aadhaar).
