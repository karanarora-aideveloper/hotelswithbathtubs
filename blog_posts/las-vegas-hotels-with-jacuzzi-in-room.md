## What to Watch Out For When Booking in Vegas

Vegas is notorious for hidden fees and misleading marketing. Keep these tips in mind:

1. **Beware the Resort Fee**: Even if you find a cheap jacuzzi suite, remember that Vegas hotels tack on a daily "Resort Fee" (often $35-$50 per night) upon arrival.
2. **Verify "Jetted" vs "Soaking"**: A "soaking tub" is just a deep bathtub. If you want bubbles and jets, ensure the description explicitly says "Whirlpool" or "Jacuzzi".
3. **Ask About the View**: A hot tub is great, but a hot tub situated next to a window overlooking the Bellagio fountains is unforgettable. Always request a high floor with a Strip view.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which Las Vegas hotels have a jacuzzi in the room?**
Top properties include ARIA (Sky Suites), The Venetian, Caesars Palace, and Elara by Hilton Grand Vacations. Many classic hotels also offer specific "Whirlpool Suites."

**How much does a jacuzzi suite cost in Las Vegas?**
Prices vary wildly based on the season and day of the week. Mid-week (Tuesday/Wednesday), you might find suites for $150-$200/night. On peak weekends or during major conventions, the same suite can easily exceed $800/night.

**Do Vegas hotels clean the jacuzzis between guests?**
Yes, reputable 4 and 5-star resorts follow strict sanitation protocols, completely flushing and sanitizing the jetted tubs between guest stays.
