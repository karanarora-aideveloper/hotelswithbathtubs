## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where can I find couple-friendly hotels with bathtubs in Kolkata?**
Most 4 and 5-star hotels in Kolkata, particularly in the Park Street, Alipore, and New Town areas, are highly couple-friendly and offer premium rooms equipped with bathtubs.

**Do 3-star hotels in Kolkata have bathtubs in the room?**
It is rare but possible. Some newer boutique 3-star properties in Salt Lake or Rajarhat feature bathtubs in their top-tier "Executive" or "Suite" categories. Always verify the specific room type before booking.

**What is the best area in Kolkata to book a luxury bathtub suite?**
For a heritage vibe, the areas around Park Street and Esplanade are best. For modern luxury and larger, newer rooms with jacuzzis, look toward EM Bypass, Salt Lake, and New Town.
