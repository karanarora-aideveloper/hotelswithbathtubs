### When is the Best Time to Book a Luxury Stay in Rishikesh?
Because Rishikesh has two peak seasons (March to May and September to November), luxury suites with private bathtubs book up very quickly. If you want a guaranteed view from your bathtub, it is highly recommended to book your stay at least 2-3 months in advance.
