## Tips for Booking a Bathtub Suite in NYC

Because NYC real estate is so expensive, finding a guaranteed bathtub requires some diligence:

1. **Beware the "Shower Only" Default**: Almost all standard rooms in NYC are shower-only. You must explicitly filter for "Bathtub" and verify the room type (usually a Deluxe Room, Corner Suite, or Penthouse).
2. **"Soaking Tub" vs. "Jacuzzi"**: True jetted jacuzzis are actually quite rare in Manhattan due to noise and plumbing regulations. Most luxury hotels opt for massive, deep "soaking tubs" instead. 
3. **Ask About Bath Amenities**: The best hotels in NYC partner with high-end apothecaries (like Le Labo or Aesop) to provide premium bath salts, oils, and bubbles. Don't hesitate to ask the concierge to set up your bath before you return from dinner.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which hotels in NYC have bathtubs with a view?**
Top choices include The William Vale (Brooklyn), The Standard High Line (Meatpacking District), and the Mandarin Oriental (Columbus Circle). These properties offer stunning tubs positioned perfectly by large windows.

**Are there cheap hotels with bathtubs in NYC?**
"Cheap" in NYC is relative, but you can sometimes find good deals at older, classic hotels in Midtown (like the New Yorker Hotel) which still have standard bathtubs in their standard rooms, though they won't be the large, luxury soaking tubs.

**How much is a luxury suite with a soaking tub in Manhattan?**
For a guaranteed deep soaking tub in a 4 or 5-star Manhattan hotel, expect to pay between $400 and $900+ per night, depending on the season and the view.
