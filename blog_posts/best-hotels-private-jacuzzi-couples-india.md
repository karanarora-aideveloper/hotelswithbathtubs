## FAQ {#faq}

### Q: What's the difference between a bathtub and a jacuzzi?
**A:** 
- **Bathtub**: Traditional tub, perfect for soaking
- **Jacuzzi**: Bath with jet system that provides massage
- Many luxury hotels offer both in one tub (soaking tub with jets)

### Q: Are all verified hotels actually good?
**A:** Our verification confirms the amenity exists, not the overall hotel quality. We recommend reading recent reviews in addition to checking our list. Look for 4+ star ratings and specific mentions of the jacuzzi/bathtub experience.

### Q: What if a hotel says it has a jacuzzi but it's not working?
**A:** Contact the hotel immediately and request a room change. Photo evidence from review sites helps prove the issue isn't just you. Most quality hotels will offer compensation or upgrade.

### Q: Can I book directly from your site?
**A:** No, we're a verification and research resource. We provide links to MakeMyTrip, Agoda, and Booking.com where you book directly at the best available rates.

### Q: How often do you update hotel information?
**A:** We verify hotels monthly and update our database when amenities change. If a hotel removes jacuzzi amenities or loses verification, we remove it from our list.

### Q: What if my budget is very tight (under ₹10,000/night)?
**A:** Verified jacuzzis at this price point are rare. We recommend:
- Looking in smaller cities (Rishikesh, Dharamshala, Nainital)
- Booking during shoulder seasons (May-June, September)
- Looking for "bathtub" rather than "jacuzzi" - still luxurious and more affordable

### Q: Which season has the best rates?
**A:** Off-season (May-June, September) offers 30-50% discounts. However, weather may not be ideal. Best value is shoulder season (March-April, October-November).

### Q: How do I verify your verification is accurate?
**A:** Great question! You can verify any hotel we list by:
1. Opening MakeMyTrip, Agoda, and Booking.com
2. Searching for the hotel
3. Checking amenities section for jacuzzi/bathtub/hot tub confirmation
4. Comparing with what we list

We encourage this - our credibility depends on accuracy!
