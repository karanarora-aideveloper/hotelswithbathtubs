Nothing spoils a romantic getaway or a luxury weekend staycation faster than checking into your hotel room only to discover a basic standing shower where you expected a deep soaking bathtub or private jacuzzi. 

Unfortunately, misleading hotel photography and vague amenity descriptions are widespread on major booking engines. Hotels frequently upload a photo of their presidential suite’s lavish marble tub to the general gallery, leaving guests in standard or deluxe rooms without a tub.

To eliminate booking uncertainty, our team uses a rigorous verification methodology. Here is the **step-by-step checklist** every traveler should follow before confirming their reservation.

---

## The 6-Step Bathtub Booking Verification Checklist

### 1. Verify the Specific Room Tier Name
Hotels rarely put bathtubs in their entry-level base rooms. When browsing MakeMyTrip, Agoda, or Booking.com, always check the exact room category name.
* Look for explicit titles like **"Deluxe Suite with Bathtub"**, **"Executive Jacuzzi Room"**, or **"Premium Mountain View with Spa Tub"**.
* Avoid selecting generic tiers labeled only as *"Standard Room"*, *"Classic Twin"*, or *"Deluxe Room"* without reading the individual room amenity bullet points.

### 2. Inspect the Dedicated Room Gallery (Not the Main Hotel Gallery)
Online travel agencies show two image collections:
* **The Main Property Gallery:** Displays the best highlights of the entire hotel (often including spa facilities or top-tier suites).
* **The Room-Specific Gallery:** Accessible by clicking directly on the room category card.
Always inspect the photos tied strictly to your chosen room tier to verify the bathtub is installed inside that exact room layout.

### 3. Check for the "In-Room Amenities" Tag
Scroll down to the room details breakdown on the booking platform. Confirm that **"Bathtub"**, **"Private Jacuzzi"**, or **"Hot Tub"** is listed under the *Room Amenities* or *Bathroom Amenities* section—not under general *Property Facilities* (which may refer to a communal health club tub).

### 4. Cross-Reference Across Multiple Platforms
Our platform uses **Triple-Source Verification** for a reason: booking platforms have varying amenity accuracy standards.
* Check the property on **MakeMyTrip** for domestic amenity tags.
* Check the same room tier on **Agoda** under the *Room Features* filter.
* Verify on **Booking.com** under *Private Bathroom Facilities*.
If all three platforms confirm the in-room bathtub, the accuracy rate is virtually 100%.

### 5. Call the Front Desk with These 3 Exact Questions
If you are booking for a special anniversary or honeymoon, a quick 60-second phone call provides total peace of mind. Ask the reservation desk:
1. *"Does the specific [Room Name] I am booking feature a private bathtub inside the bathroom, or is it a shower only?"*
2. *"Is the tub a deep soaking tub or a jet/jacuzzi tub?"*
3. *"Does the room have a dedicated high-capacity continuous water geyser/boiler to easily fill the tub with hot water?"*

### 6. Add a Written Special Request on Your Booking Voucher
When completing checkout on MakeMyTrip, Agoda, or Booking.com, use the **Special Requests** box to write:
> *"Traveling for a romantic celebration. Please assign a room that includes the confirmed in-room private bathtub as described in this booking. Thank you!"*

While special requests are subject to availability at check-in, specifying this in writing creates an official record on your reservation voucher.
