Raaj Resort Panvel

Hotel with Bathtub in Panvel

Address: Usarli Budruk Near Ritghar, Panvel, 410206 Panvel, India

Check Availability

Situated in Panvel, 45 km from Phoenix Market City Mall, Raaj Resort features accommodation with an outdoor swimming pool, free private parking, a garden and a shared lounge. With a terrace, the 3-star resort has air-conditioned rooms with free WiFi, each with a private bathroom. The accommodation provides room service, a 24-hour front desk and currency exchange for guests.

At the resort, rooms have a desk and a flat-screen TV. Rooms are equipped with a coffee machine, while some rooms have a balcony and others also boast garden views. At Raaj Resort all rooms have bed linen and towels.

Breakfast is available each morning, and includes buffet, Full English/Irish and halal options. At the accommodation you will find a restaurant serving Indian cuisine. A halal option can also be requested. Raaj Resort offers a children’s playground.

Dadar Railway Station is 48 km from the resort, while Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay is 49 km from the property. The nearest airport is Chhatrapati Shivaji International Mumbai, 45 km from Raaj Resort, and the property offers a paid airport shuttle service.
