Frequently Asked Questions

Are the bathtubs and jacuzzis totally private?

Yes! Every hotel featured in our directory offers private, in-room bathtubs or jacuzzis. They are located inside your personal suite, ensuring complete privacy for your romantic getaway.

How do I verify the room amenities before booking?

When you click 'Check Availability' on any of our destination pages, you will be redirected to the official booking partner. Always ensure you select the specific room type (e.g., "Suite with Spa Bath" or "Jacuzzi Suite") during checkout, as standard rooms in the same hotel may only have showers.

Which destinations are best for couples?

Popular choices for a romantic retreat include Goa, Udaipur, Lonavala, and Nainital, as they offer stunning views (beaches, lakes, or mountains) right from your private tub.
