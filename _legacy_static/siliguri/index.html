Check Availability

MAYFAIR Tea Resort features free bikes, outdoor swimming pool, a fitness centre and garden in Siliguri. Featuring a shared lounge, this 5-star hotel has air-conditioned rooms with a private bathroom. The accommodation provides evening entertainment and room service.

At the hotel, the rooms have a desk. Each room has a kettle, a flat-screen TV and a safety deposit box, while selected rooms come with a balcony and some have mountain views. At MAYFAIR Tea Resort every room comes with bed linen and towels.

A buffet, continental or Italian breakfast can be enjoyed at the property. At the accommodation you will find a restaurant serving American, Chinese and Indian cuisine. Vegetarian and dairy-free options can also be requested.

MAYFAIR Tea Resort offers a sun terrace. You can play billiards at the hotel, and car hire is available.

Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary is 3.6 km from MAYFAIR Tea Resort, while Darjeeling Himalayan Toy Train Railway is 6.2 km away. The nearest airport is Bagdogra International, 14 km from the accommodation, and the property offers a paid airport shuttle service.
