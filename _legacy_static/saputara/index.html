Hotel Lake View

Hotel with Bathtub in Room in Saputara

Address: Block No.4, Dang, Saputara Dist, 394720 Saputara, India

Check Availability

Offering an outdoor swimming pool, a fitness centre and a spa and wellness centre, Hotel Lake View is located in Saputara, just 100 metres from the Saputara Lake. Free WiFi access is available in the public areas of the property.

Each air-conditioned room here will provide you with a satellite TV and a minibar. Featuring a shower, private bathroom also comes with a bath and free toiletries. You can enjoy mountain view from the room.

At Hotel Lake View you will find a 24-hour front desk and garden. Other facilities offered at the property include a games room, a tour desk and a children’s playground. The property offers free parking.

The Saputara Bus Station is 100 metres, the Nashik Road Railway Station is 70 km and the Chhatrapati Shivaji International Airport is 200 km.

Dining option includes Sugar n Spice that serves vegetarian multi-cuisine and Deevan E Khaas, a fine dine vegetarian restaurant that serves Indian, Chinese and continental delights.
